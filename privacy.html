Train Masters: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 (Google Play added)

Train Masters is a railway-building game for Amazon Fire TV and Fire tablets, Android phones, tablets and TV (Google Play), and web browsers. We've written this policy in plain English. In short: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your railways, settings and progress are saved only on your own device, in the game's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The free browser version

The free version you can play on this website works the same way: your railway is saved only in your own web browser (its local storage), never sent anywhere. It uses no cookies, and it has no purchases: Pro and extra engines are only in the store versions (Amazon Appstore and Google Play).

No accounts, no tracking, no adverts

There are no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind. The game works without an internet connection.

Purchases

The optional extras ("Train Masters Pro", the Heritage Collection and single engines) are bought through the Amazon Appstore or Google Play, depending on where you got the game. The store handles the payment and tells the game only which extras are unlocked. We never see your name, address or payment details. The store's own privacy notice covers that purchase.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the Train Masters page on the Amazon Appstore or Google Play.
