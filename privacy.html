Train Masters: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Train Masters is a railway-building game for Amazon Fire TV. We've written this policy in plain English. In short: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your railways, settings and progress are saved only on your own device, in the game's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

No accounts, no tracking, no adverts

There are no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind. The game works without an internet connection.

Purchases

The optional "Train Masters Pro" upgrade is bought through the Amazon Appstore. Amazon handles the payment and tells the game only whether Pro is unlocked. We never see your name, address or payment details. Amazon's own privacy notice covers that purchase.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the Train Masters page on the Amazon Appstore.
